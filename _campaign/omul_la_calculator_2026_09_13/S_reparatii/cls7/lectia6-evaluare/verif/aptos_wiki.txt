'''Aptos''', originally named '''Bierstadt''', is a [[sans-serif]] [[typeface]] in the [[Neo-grotesque sans-serif|neo-grotesque style]] developed by [[Steve Matteson]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Warren |first1=Tom |title=Meet Microsoft Office's new default font: Aptos |url=https://www.theverge.com/2023/7/13/23793428/microsoft-aptos-new-default-font-office-365 |website=The Verge |date=13 July 2023 |access-date=14 July 2023}}</ref> In 2023, it became the new default font for the [[Microsoft Office]] suite, replacing the previously used [[Calibri]] font.<ref name=":0" />

Aptos, as well as its variants Aptos Display, Aptos Narrow, Aptos Mono (a [[monospaced font]]), Aptos Serif ([[serif font]]), and Aptos Slab ([[Slab serif|Slab serif font]]), are available to [[Microsoft 365]] subscribers via Microsoft's cloud service.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |date=6 November 2023 |title=Cloud fonts in Office |url=https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/cloud-fonts-in-office-f7b009fe-037f-45ed-a556-b5fe6ede6adb |access-date=18 January 2024 |website=Microsoft}}</ref>

== Characteristics ==
Aptos is available in the following styles: Light, Light Italic, Regular, Italic, Semibold, Semibold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, Extrabold, Extrabold Italic, Black, and Black Italic.

The typeface has several distinct characteristics which make the typeface easily readable.<ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Daniels |first=Si |date=28 February 2024 |title=Behind the design: A deep dive into Aptos |url=https://medium.com/microsoft-design/behind-the-design-a-deep-dive-into-aptos-d1ad5b900748 |url-status= |access-date=2 March 2024 |website=Medium}}</ref>

* It has horizontal and vertical stroke endings based on [[Helvetica]] as opposed to the slanted stroke ending of [[Arial]].
* It removed the distinctive tail of the lowercase letter "a" and added it to the lowercase letter "l" (L), as in the lowercase letter "t", which prevents [[homoglyph]] confusion with the uppercase letter "I" (i).
* The shapes of the uppercase letters in "O" and "R" and the lowercase letter "a" are slightly irregular.
* It has a double-story lowercase letter "g" with an angled stem instead of the single-story letter, as in Helvetica, and the uppercase letter "G" is rounded and has no spur.
* Little swing tail of uppercase letter "R".
* Lowercase letters "b", "c", and "p" and the uppercase letter "C" have wide contours.
* It has circular dot in [[Punctuation|punctuations]], [[diacritic]]s, and [[tittle]]s in lowercase letters "i" and "j" as opposed to the square dots in Arial and Helvetica.
* The curved top flag of the numeral "1" is based on Arial.
* Slanted stem of the numeral "7".
* The curved tail of the uppercase letter "Q" meets its [[counter (typography)|counter]].

Aptos includes characters from Latin, [[Latin script in Unicode]], [[Greek alphabet|Greek]], and [[Cyrillic script]]. The [[Italic type]] of Aptos have been individually redrawn, rather than mechanically slanted. The italic does not have cursive forms except Cyrillic scripts; instead, the letter shapes are [[Oblique type|oblique]] forms of the upright letters, as opposed to the true italic form of Calibri.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Microsoft Learn |date=20 January 2024 |title=Aptos Font Family |url=https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/font-list/aptos |access-date=3 March 2024}}</ref>

== History ==
Aptos was originally named Bierstadt, after [[Mount Bierstadt]] of the [[Rocky Mountains]] in [[Colorado]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Matteson |first=Steve |date=13 May 2021 |title=A Closer Look at Bierstadt (the new font design – not 'Beer-Town' |url=https://mattesontypographics.com/blog/f/a-closer-look-at-bierstadt-the-new-fonts-%E2%80%93-not-%E2%80%98beer-town%E2%80%99 |access-date=2 March 2024 |website=Matteson Typographics |language=en-US |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240301185617/https://mattesontypographics.com/blog/f/a-closer-look-at-bierstadt-the-new-fonts-%E2%80%93-not-%E2%80%98beer-town%E2%80%99 |archive-date=1 March 2024 |url-status=dead}}</ref> It is based on typical [[Switzerland|Swiss]] fonts from the mid-20th century. The font was intended to evoke a sense of the Helvetica and Arial fonts.<ref name=":1" /> It was introduced in 2021 alongside four other fonts (Grandview, Seaford, Skeena and Tenorite) for the Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365 applications. As a result, Bierstadt was considered a potential successor to the Microsoft Office standard font Calibri.<ref>{{Cite web |date=28 April 2021 |title=Beyond Calibri: Finding Microsoft's next default font |url=https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2021/04/28/beyond-calibri-finding-microsofts-next-default-font/ |access-date=16 July 2023 |website=Microsoft 365 Blog |publisher=Microsoft}}</ref>

In July 2023, Microsoft announced that Bierstadt would succeed Calibri, although it would be renamed after [[Aptos, California]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Daniels |first=Si |date=13 July 2023 |title=A change of typeface: Microsoft's new default font has arrived |url=https://medium.com/microsoft-design/a-change-of-typeface-microsofts-new-default-font-has-arrived-f200eb16718d |access-date=16 July 2023 |website=Medium}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Valentino |first=Silas |date=May 23, 2024 |title=Aptos is world famous, but nobody in the Calif. town knows why |url=https://www.sfgate.com/centralcoast/article/aptos-coastal-calif-profile-microsoft-typeface-19468314.php |access-date=2024-05-23 |work=SFGATE |language=en}}</ref> It has been argued that Calibri had a "friendly" feeling that could skew writer intent, whereas Aptos could display a writer's words in a "clear and neutral" tone.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Parakala |first=Vangmayi |date=5 Apr 2024 |title=The arrival of Aptos and the fuss about fonts |url=https://www.livemint.com/mint-lounge/ideas/calibri-vs-aptos-steve-matteson-microsoft-office-default-font-font-psychology-new-typeface-design-111712252853111.html |access-date=8 February 2025 |magazine=[[Mint (newspaper)|Lounge]] |publisher=[[HT Media]]}}</ref>

== Availability ==
Aptos, Aptos Display, Aptos Narrow, Aptos Mono, Aptos Serif, and Aptos Slab, along with the original Bierstadt, Grandview, Seaford, Skeena and Tenorite are available to [[Microsoft 365]] subscribers via Microsoft's cloud service.<ref name=":3" /> A locally installed version of Aptos is also available from Microsoft.<ref>{{Cite web |date=6 April 2024 |title=Microsoft Aptos Fonts |url=https://www.microsoft.com/download/details.aspx?id=106087 |website=Download Center |publisher=Microsoft}}</ref>

== Criticisms ==
Aptos has been known to have issues with [[kerning|collision avoidance]] in word processors when using italicized and non-italicized words in the same sentence.{{Citation needed|date=March 2026}}
