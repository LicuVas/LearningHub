<div class="challenge-step-text">
                            Deschide un fisier Excel nou. In celula <strong>A1</strong> scrie "Elev", iar in <strong>B1</strong> scrie "Nota". In celulele <strong>B2:B9</strong> scrie aceste note: <code>7</code>, <code>9</code>, <code>5</code>, <code>10</code>, <code>8</code>, <code>6</code>, <code>4</code>, <code>9</code>.
                        </div>
</div>

<span class="challenge-step-num">2</span>
<div class="challenge-step-text">

                        </div>
</div>
<div class="challenge-step">
<span class="challenge-step-num">4</span>
<div class="challenge-step-text">

                        </div>
</div>
<div class="challenge-step">
<span class="challenge-step-num">5</span>

                        </div>
