            </div>
            <div class="atom-content">
                <div class="concept-name">MIN si MAX: Valorile extreme</div>
<div class="concept-def">
<p><strong>MIN</strong> cauta si returneaza cea mai mica valoare numerica dintr-un interval. Ignora celulele cu text sau celulele goale.</p>
</div>
<div class="analogy-box">
<strong>🏆 Analogie: Clasamentul sportiv</strong>
<p>Imaginati-va ca aveti timpii de alergare ai colegilor la ora de sport: 12,5s, 11,2s, 13,8s, 10,9s, 12,1s. MIN gaseste cel mai mic timp (10,9s - cel mai rapid), iar MAX gaseste cel mai mare timp (13,8s - cel mai lent).</p>
<p><strong>Atentie:</strong> La alergare, valoarea mica = performanta buna (mai rapid). La <em>note scolare</em> este invers: MIN returneaza nota cea mai mica (performanta slaba), iar MAX returneaza nota cea mai mare (performanta buna). MIN si MAX gasesc intotdeauna valorile numerice extreme &mdash; sensul (bun/slab) depinde de ce reprezinta acele valori.</p>
</div>
<div class="warning-box" style="border-left: none;">
<div class="warning-box-title" style="border-left: none;">⚠ Calculatorul tau poate fi setat diferit.</div>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); font-size: 0.9rem; line-height: 1.7;">
                            Pe unele calculatoare Excel desparte argumentele cu <strong>punct si virgula</strong> (<code>;</code>), pe altele cu <strong>virgula</strong> (<code>,</code>). Daca formula ta da eroare, incearca celalalt semn. La fel la zecimale: pe unele scrii <code>7,5</code>, pe altele <code>7.5</code>.
                        </p>
</div>
<div class="code-label code-label-red">Exemplu MIN si MAX</div>
<div class="code-block"><span class="cmt">// Note in celulele B2:B9: 7, 9, 5, 10, 8, 6, 4, 9</span>
<span class="func">=MIN</span>(<span class="range">B2:B9</span>)        <span class="cmt">// Rezultat: 4  (nota cea mai mica)</span>
<span class="func">=MAX</span>(<span class="range">B2:B9</span>)        <span class="cmt">// Rezultat: 10 (nota cea mai mare)</span>
<span class="cmt">// Amplitudinea (diferenta intre max si min):</span>
<span class="func">=MAX</span>(<span class="range">B2:B9</span>) - <span class="func">MIN</span>(<span class="range">B2:B9</span>)  <span class="cmt">// Rezultat: 6 (10 - 4)</span>
<span class="cmt">// Poti specifica si celule separate:</span>
<span class="func">=MIN</span>(<span class="range">B2; B5; B8</span>)   <span class="cmt">// Minimul dintre doar 3 celule (sau cu virgule: =MIN(B2, B5, B8))</span></div>

            <div class="atom-header">
                <div class="atom-number">4</div>
                <h3 class="atom-title">AVERAGE: Calculeaza media aritmetica</h3>
            </div>
            <div class="atom-content">
                <div class="concept-name">AVERAGE: Calculeaza media aritmetica</div>
<div class="concept-def">
<p><strong>AVERAGE</strong> aduna toate valorile numerice si le imparte la numarul lor. In Excel se scrie <strong>AVERAGE</strong> si pe calculatoarele cu Office in romana (numele functiilor nu se traduc; nu exista "MEDIA" sau "AVG").</p>
<p>Formula matematica: Media = (Suma valorilor) / (Numarul de valori)</p>
</div>
<div class="analogy-box">
<strong>🎓 Analogie: Media la scoala</strong>
<p>Stii cum iti calculeaza profesorul media semestriala? Aduna toate notele si imparte la numarul lor. Exact asta face AVERAGE! Daca ai notele 7, 9, 5, 10, 8, 6, 4, 9 (total: 58), media e 58/8 = 7,25. Cu Excel scrii doar <code style="background:rgba(0,0,0,0.3);padding:0.1rem 0.4rem;border-radius:4px;font-family:Consolas,monospace;color:#06b6d4;">=AVERAGE(B2:B9)</code> si primesti 7,25 (sau 7.25, dupa calculator) instant.</p>
</div>
<div class="code-label code-label-red">Exemplu AVERAGE</div>
<div class="code-block"><span class="cmt">// Note in B2:B9: 7, 9, 5, 10, 8, 6, 4, 9</span>
<span class="func">=AVERAGE</span>(<span class="range">B2:B9</span>)     <span class="cmt">// Rezultat: 7,25</span>
<span class="cmt">// Calcul: (7+9+5+10+8+6+4+9) / 8 = 58 / 8 = 7,25</span>
<span class="cmt">// AVERAGE ignora celulele goale:</span>
<span class="cmt">// Daca B9 e goala, AVERAGE calculeaza (7+9+5+10+8+6+4) / 7 = 49 / 7 = 7</span>
<span class="cmt">// Nu imparte la 8, ci la 7 (cate numere sunt de fapt)</span></div>
<div class="visual-box" style="border-left: none;">
<div class="visual-title">Cum functioneaza =AVERAGE(B2:B9) pas cu pas</div>
<div style="text-align: center; padding: 0.5rem;">
<div style="display: inline-block; background: var(--bg-primary); border-radius: 10px; padding: 1rem 1.5rem; margin: 0.25rem;">
<div style="font-size: 0.8rem; color: var(--text-muted); margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Pasul 1: Aduna toate valorile</div>
<div style="font-family: Consolas, monospace; font-size: 1rem;">
<span style="color: var(--accent-cyan);">7 + 9 + 5 + 10 + 8 + 6 + 4 + 9</span> = <span style="color: var(--accent-orange); font-weight: 700;">58</span>
</div>
</div>
<div style="font-size: 1.5rem; color: var(--accent-rose); font-weight: 800; margin: 0.5rem 0;">↓</div>
<div style="display: inline-block; background: var(--bg-primary); border-radius: 10px; padding: 1rem 1.5rem; margin: 0.25rem;">
<div style="font-size: 0.8rem; color: var(--text-muted); margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Pasul 2: Imparte la numarul de valori</div>
<div style="font-family: Consolas, monospace; font-size: 1rem;">
<span style="color: var(--accent-orange);">58</span> / <span style="color: var(--accent-purple);">8 valori</span> = <span style="color: var(--accent-green); font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.2rem;">7,25</span>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="diagram-footer">AVERAGE = SUM / COUNT. E ca si cum ai folosi doua functii combinate intr-una singura!</div>
</div>
<div class="warning-box" style="border-left: none;">
<div class="warning-box-title" style="border-left: none;">⚠ Atentie: AVERAGE si celulele goale</div>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); font-size: 0.9rem; line-height: 1.7;">
                            AVERAGE <strong>ignora</strong> celulele goale! Daca ai 8 celule dar 2 sunt goale, AVERAGE imparte suma la 6, nu la 8. Aceasta e diferit de a pune 0 in celulele goale - cu 0, media ar fi mai mica.
                        </p>
</div>
