<div class="concept-definition">
<strong>Definitie:</strong> Animation Pane este un panou lateral care arata toate animatiile de pe slide-ul curent, permitandu-ti sa le modifici, reorganizezi si configurezi in detaliu.
                </div>
<h4 style="color: var(--accent-blue); margin: 1.5rem 0 1rem 0;">Cum deschizi Animation Pane:</h4>
<div class="code-block">
<button class="copy-btn">Copiaza</button>
<pre>Metoda 1: Animations tab → Animation Pane (buton in dreapta)
Metoda 2: Click dreapta pe o animatie → Show Animation Pane
Metoda 3: Animations tab → Advanced Animation group → Animation Pane</pre>
</div>
<h4 style="color: var(--accent-blue); margin: 1.5rem 0 1rem 0;">Ce poti face in Animation Pane:</h4>
<ul style="list-style: none; padding-left: 0;">
<li style="padding: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; background: var(--bg-secondary); border-radius: 6px;">📋 <strong>Vezi toate animatiile:</strong> Lista completa numerotata in ordinea in care ruleaza</li>
<li style="padding: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; background: var(--bg-secondary); border-radius: 6px;">🔄 <strong>Reorganizeaza ordinea:</strong> Drag &amp; drop pentru a schimba secventa</li>
<li style="padding: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; background: var(--bg-secondary); border-radius: 6px;">⏱️ <strong>Schimba temporizarea:</strong> Click pe sageata → Timing pentru secunde exacte</li>
<li style="padding: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; background: var(--bg-secondary); border-radius: 6px;">🎬 <strong>Preview animatii:</strong> Click pe "Play" pentru a vedea efectul</li>
<li style="padding: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; background: var(--bg-secondary); border-radius: 6px;">⚙️ <strong>Setari avansate:</strong> Click dreapta → Effect Options pentru configurari detaliate</li>
</ul>
<div class="example-box">
<h4>📋 Exemplu: Cum arata Animation Pane</h4>
<div style="background: var(--bg-primary); padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 0.9rem;">
<div style="background: var(--success); color: white; padding: 0.25rem 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem; border-radius: 4px;">1 ▼ Titlu slide (Fade)</div>
<div style="background: var(--success); color: white; padding: 0.25rem 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem; border-radius: 4px;">2 ▼ Punct 1 (Fly In)</div>
<div style="background: var(--success); color: white; padding: 0.25rem 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem; border-radius: 4px;">3 ▼ Punct 2 (Fly In)</div>
<div style="background: var(--warning); color: white; padding: 0.25rem 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.25rem; border-radius: 4px;">4 ▼ Imagine (Zoom)</div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; color: var(--text-secondary);">
<strong>Explicatie:</strong> Verde = Entrance, Galben = Emphasis. Numerele arata ordinea. Sagetile permit accesul la setari.
                    </p>
</div>
<div class="info-box">
                    Animation Pane devine esential cand ai mai mult de 2-3 animatii pe slide. Te ajuta sa vizualizezi si controlezi toata secventa.
                </div>

            <div class="atom-content">
                <div class="concept-header">
<div class="concept-icon">⏱️</div>
<div class="concept-title">Timing si Trigger - Cand si cum pornesc animatiile</div>
</div>
<div class="concept-definition">
<strong>Definitie:</strong> Timing controleaza CAND si CUM porneste o animatie. Trigger-ul defineste CE declanseaza animatia (click, automat, sau la click pe un obiect specific).
                </div>
<h4 style="color: var(--accent-blue); margin: 1.5rem 0 1rem 0;">Optiuni de START (Start trigger):</h4>
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
<div style="background: var(--bg-secondary); padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-blue);">
<strong style="color: var(--accent-blue);">1. On Click (Default)</strong>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-top: 0.5rem;">Animatia porneste cand faci click (sau apeși pe tastatura) in timpul prezentarii. Aceasta este cea mai folosita optiune pentru control total.</p>
<p style="color: var(--text-muted); margin-top: 0.5rem; font-size: 0.9rem;">💡 Foloseste pentru: Prezentari live unde vrei sa controlezi ritmul</p>
</div>
<div style="background: var(--bg-secondary); padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-green);">
<strong style="color: var(--accent-green);">2. With Previous</strong>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-top: 0.5rem;">Porneste simultan cu animatia anterioara. Utila pentru efecte combinate (ex: text apare + imagine se mareste in acelasi timp).</p>
<p style="color: var(--text-muted); margin-top: 0.5rem; font-size: 0.9rem;">💡 Foloseste pentru: Animatii sincronizate, efecte complexe</p>
</div>
<div style="background: var(--bg-secondary); padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid var(--warning);">
<strong style="color: var(--warning);">3. After Previous</strong>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-top: 0.5rem;">Porneste automat dupa ce animatia anterioara se termina. Creeaza o secventa automata fara click-uri.</p>
<p style="color: var(--text-muted); margin-top: 0.5rem; font-size: 0.9rem;">💡 Foloseste pentru: Prezentari automate, kioskuri, storytelling fluid</p>
</div>
</div>
<h4 style="color: var(--accent-blue); margin: 1.5rem 0 1rem 0;">Duration (Durata):</h4>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-bottom: 1rem;">Cat de mult timp dureaza animatia de la inceput la sfarsit.</p>
<ul style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-left: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">
<li><strong>Scurt (0.5 secunde):</strong> Efect rapid, energic - bun pentru butoane, elemente mici</li>
<li><strong>Mediu (1 secunda - DEFAULT):</strong> Echilibrat pentru majoritatea situatiilor</li>
<li><strong>Lung (2+ secunde):</strong> Efect dramatic, lent - pentru momente importante</li>
</ul>
<h4 style="color: var(--accent-blue); margin: 1.5rem 0 1rem 0;">Delay (Intarziere):</h4>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-bottom: 1rem;">Cat timp asteapta inainte sa porneasca (dupa trigger). Exemplu: Delay 1s = asteapta 1 secunda apoi porneste.</p>
<div class="example-box">
<h4>🎬 Exemplu practic: Secventa de animatie</h4>
<p style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-bottom: 1rem;">Vrei ca pe slide sa apara: (1) Titlu, apoi (2) Imagine, apoi (3) Text explicativ</p>
<div style="background: var(--bg-primary); padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px;">
<div style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
<strong style="color: var(--accent-blue);">Animatia 1: Titlu</strong>
<div style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-left: 1rem; margin-top: 0.25rem;">
                                • Tip: Fade (Entrance)<br/>
                                • Start: On Click<br/>
                                • Duration: 1s
                            </div>
</div>
<div style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;">
<strong style="color: var(--accent-green);">Animatia 2: Imagine</strong>
<div style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-left: 1rem; margin-top: 0.25rem;">
                                • Tip: Zoom (Entrance)<br/>
                                • Start: After Previous<br/>
                                • Duration: 0.75s<br/>
                                • Delay: 0.5s (asteapta 0.5s dupa titlu)
                            </div>
</div>
<div>
<strong style="color: var(--warning);">Animatia 3: Text</strong>
<div style="color: var(--text-secondary); margin-left: 1rem; margin-top: 0.25rem;">
                                • Tip: Fly In (Entrance)<br/>
                                • Start: After Previous<br/>
                                • Duration: 1s
                            </div>
</div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; color: var(--text-secondary);">
<strong>Rezultat:</strong> Click → Titlu apare (1s) → Pauza 0.5s → Imagine apare (0.75s) → Text zboara inauntru (1s). Total: 3.25 secunde automata dupa primul click!
                    </p>
</div>
<div class="tip-box">
                    Pentru prezentari live, foloseste On Click pentru controlul complet. Pentru prezentari automate (kioskuri, expozitii), foloseste After Previous si Delay pentru secvente fluide.
                </div>
<div class="warning-box">
                    Atentie la animatii prea lungi! O durata de peste 2 secunde poate parea lenta si plictisitoare. Tine-le scurte si dinamice (0.5-1.5s).
                </div>
