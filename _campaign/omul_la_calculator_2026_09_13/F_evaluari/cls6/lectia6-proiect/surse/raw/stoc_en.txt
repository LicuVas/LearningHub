Show me how I'm stuck on icons: A new kind of Office clip Microsoft 365 subscribers have access to a growing library of thousands of royalty-free images, icons, illustrations, videos, and more to use in their documents, presentations, workbooks, and emails. Currently available in Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, with more apps coming soon. New content is added monthly, so stay tuned. Note Office 2021 and Office for the web users who are not Microsoft 365 subscribers, will have access to a portion of the library. To get the full library, sign in with your Microsoft 365 account or subscribe to Microsoft 365 . Try it On the Insert tab, select Pictures and then Stock Images. Select one or more items from Images , Icons , Cutout People , Stickers , Videos (PPT only), Illustrations or Cartoon People .
