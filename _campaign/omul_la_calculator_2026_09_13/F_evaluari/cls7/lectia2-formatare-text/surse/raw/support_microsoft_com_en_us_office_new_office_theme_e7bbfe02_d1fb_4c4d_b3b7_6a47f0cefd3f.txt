New Office theme
Applies To Microsoft 365
Get help with Copilot
Tell me about new Office theme.
How should I switch or remove a theme?
Show me how to change theme effects.
The new Office Theme is on its way and will soon be available to all Office users. This new look specifies an updated standard color palette and type font that are applied throughout Microsoft Office. The new theme will affect only your document, not the look and feel of Microsoft 365.
Default Office font
Aptos is the default font across all of Office. Learn more about the history of Aptos here . The Aptos font is also available for download here .

Default Office theme colors
The color palette of the Office theme prioritizes the creation of modern and accessible content. The previous Office theme has been renamed Office Theme 2013–2022 and can still be accessed in the theme galleries of each Office application.
