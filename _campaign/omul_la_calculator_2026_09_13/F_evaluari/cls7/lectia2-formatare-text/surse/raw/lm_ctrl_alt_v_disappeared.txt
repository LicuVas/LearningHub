Microsoft has changed Ctrl + Alt + V shortcut to paste formatting. Its companion is Crtl + Alt + C to copy formatting. Alt+HVS will open the Paste Special dialog.
You can create a new keyboard shortcut:
Choose File>Options>Customize Ribbon .
Beside Keyboard shortcuts , click on the Customize button.
In the left-hand list of Categories , select All Commands .
In the right-hand list of Commands , choose EditPasteSpecial .
Click in the Press new shortcut key field, then press your new key combination. If a Currently assigned to message appears, you may want to choose a new combination to avoid a conflict.
Click on the Assign button, then OK out.
Ctrl+Alt+V has always been there. It can't be changed/reassigned, however. The buttons are grayed out.
Unfortunately, Microsoft and most vendors make too many changes, too often. They are unwarranted most of the time. They are also intrusive and obtrusive. People use many shortcuts like forever. All of a sudden, some shortcuts stop working. It was Microsoft who created a great shortcut, Ctrl+F to find on a page. It was changed to something else in Word. Too bad!
Now, Microsoft did it again. It disabled the handy Ctrl+Alt+V. I don't like to do extra moves instead (clicking on the Paste button on the Home tab, then choose paste option).
I think I know how this insane change occurred. It is because of a recent feature in the Edge browser. The shortcuts craze added Shift+Alt+V as paste without formatting. The unwarranted effect is that it affected all Microsoft applications! That's unacceptable!

Hi Ion,
check this one out:
Unassign the pre-existing Alt+Ctrl+V from EditPasteSpecial by assigning it to another command (e.g., PasteTextOnly ).
Close the Shortcut Customization and Word Options pop-ups to save changes.
Manually reassign Alt+Ctrl+V to EditPasteSpecial .
That’s it—your beloved Paste Special shortcut should now work! Please try it and let us know.
Cheers,
G
Detailed Answer:
This issue has been frustrating me for a while, so I completely understand and agree to your points about MS and other providers.
After some trial and error, I stumbled upon a fix that restored Alt+Ctrl+V shortcut for the Paste Special pop-up.
Here’s what I discovered:
When you open "Customize Shortcuts" and search for the EditPasteSpecial command in the "Home" category, it shows Alt+Ctrl+V as an existing shortcut—but it doesn’t work! Worse, the option to remove doesn't work either (as you pointed out)
To work around this, I first assigned Alt+Ctrl+V as an additional shortcut to the PasteTextOnly function (which originally only had Ctrl+Shift+V ). This indirectly removed Alt+Ctrl+V from EditPasteSpecial .
I tried the shortcuts and they worked... but later I decided to undo this change.
I re-assigned Alt+Ctrl+V back to EditPasteSpecial .
Voilà — Paste Special shortcut was working again!
I hope this helps!

At least in recent versions of Word for Windows, Ctrl+Shift+V is a new shortcut for pasting in plain text format.
Paste formatting has been changed to Ctrl+Alt+V (to be used in conjunction with Ctrl+Alt+C which copies formatting from the selection).
For more, see https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/microsoft-365-insider-blog/paste-text-only-shortcut-in-word/ba-p/4218362 .
