Change the color, size, or format of bullets or numbers in a list in Word
Applies To Word for Microsoft 365 Word 2024 Word 2021 Word 2019 Word 2016
Get help with Copilot
How would I choose a new bullet or numbering format?
Show me how to format a bulleted or numbered list in Word
How can I convert bullets to numbers or vice versa?
To change the formatting of the bullets or numbers in a list, select any bullet or number to select all the bullets or numbers in the list. If you select the text, the formatting of both the text and the bullets or numbering changes.
Choose a new bullet or numbering format
Select a bullet or number in the list that you want to change.
In a multiple-level list, you can change the formatting one level at a time by selecting one bullet or number at that level in the list.
On the Home tab, under Paragraph , select the arrow next to Bullets or Numbering .
Select the bullet or numbering list format that you want in the Bullet Library or the Numbering Library .
Define a new bullet character, font, or picture
Bullets shown in the bullet library are a subset of the Word font libraries. This means you can choose a different character for your bullets. For example, you can change your dot bullets to arrows or even something like a Euro or Dollar character. You can also use an image either found through the internet, or on your computer.
Select a bullet in a list to select all the bullets.
Select the down arrow next to the bullet icon.
.
At the bottom of the pop-up panel, select Define New Bullet .
In the Define New Bullet panel, do one of the following:
To change or add a character , select Symbol . From the symbol library, choose a character and select OK .
To change the font attributes after picking a new character, select Font . Change the style, size, and font, and then select OK .
To add a picture, select Picture . In the Insert Pictures window, select either From a file for images on your computer, or Bing Image Search to find a picture online.
Choose the image you want to use from your computer, or a Bing search, and then select Open or Insert .
If your new bullets look good in the Preview , then select OK .
Change the font format, color, or size for bullets or numbers in a list
Bullet and number fonts, colors, and size are all controlled by the Font attributes. You can change the text formatting of bullets or numbers in a list without making changes to the text in the list. For example, you can format numbers or bullets with a different font color than the text in the list.
Select a bullet or number in a list.
All the bullets or numbers in the list are selected.
On the Home tab, in the Font group, make the changes that you want.
For example, select the arrow next to Font Color , and then select the color that you want.
Change the level of a bulleted or numbered items in my list
You can easily change the level of a list item.
Select the bullet or number that has moved out of position.
On the Home tab, under Paragraph , select the arrow next to Bullets or Numbering .
Point to Change List Level , and then select the level that you want.
Convert bullets to numbers or vice versa in a list
Select a bullet or number in the list that you want to change.
By selecting a bullet or number in an automatically recognized bulleted or numbered list, you select the entire list.
On the Home tab, in the Paragraph group, select Bullets or Numbering .
To change the list back, select either Bullets
or Numbering
.
Convert bullets to numbers or vice versa in a multilevel list
A multilevel list shows the list items at several levels rather than at one level.
Select a bullet or number in the list at the level that you want to change.
By selecting a bullet or number in an automatically recognized bulleted or numbered list, you select all of the list items that are at that particular level.
To convert the entire multilevel list to bullets or numbers, select the entire list.
On the Home tab, in the Paragraph group, select Bullets or Numbering .
To change the list back, select Bullets
or Numbering
.
