guides Logos Flyers Banners Posters Resumes Photo collages YouTube thumbnails All create guides Canva home Inviting your students to Canva Education Frequently Asked Questions Who can I invite to my Canva Education brand? You can invite your students, your co-teacher, and other school staff (including librarians and curriculum coordinators). Canva Education is not currently available for college and university students and staff, but they can still use Canva for free. Is Canva Education safe to use with students? Yes. Our dedicated education and district offerings are FERPA and COPPA compliant, and offers safe-for-school content. We are also a signatory of the National Data Privacy Agreement. Do I need parental permission before inviting my students? Teachers need to ensure they have parental consent for children below 13. More on this here ⁠ (opens in a new tab or window) . Will my students be given their own Canva login? Yes. Your students will have their own Canva login, and they will be added as a member of your Canva Education brand. Will my students have access to my personal designs on Canva? No. Only the designs you share with your students, either directly or via your shared classroom space, will be accessible by your students. All of your other designs will remain private. Will my students be able to invite others to the Canva Education brand? No, students are not able to invite other members into the brand. What do other teachers do with their students on Canva? Visit our Design School resources ⁠ (opens in a new tab or window) to learn more about how teachers from around the world are creating, communicating and engaging their students with Canva Education.
