SURSA (text brut, curl 13.09.2026 -> surse/raw/mhz.html -> extrage.py -> surse/mhz.txt): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megahertz_myth

CITAT 1: The megahertz myth , or in more recent cases the gigahertz myth , refers to the misconception of only using clock rate (for example measured in megahertz or gigahertz) to compare the performance of different microprocessors .

CITAT 2: While clock rates are a valid way of comparing the performance of different speeds of the same model and type of processor, other factors such as an amount of execution units , pipeline depth , cache hierarchy , branch prediction , and instruction sets can greatly affect the performance when considering different processors.

CITAT 3: In contrast, the 6502, which has a much simpler fetch-execute pipeline, always takes the same number of clock cycles to execute a given instruction in any context.

CONCLUZIE: GHz = frecventa ceasului procesorului (cate „batai” pe secunda), nu numarul de operatii. O instructiune poate lua mai multe batai (citatul 3), iar alti factori schimba mult viteza reala (citatul 2). „3 GHz = 3 miliarde de operatii/secunda” este chiar confuzia numita „gigahertz myth” (citatul 1). Sursa e secundara (Wikipedia), dar faptul e standard in arhitectura calculatoarelor.
Formulare corecta pentru clasa a V-a: „GHz arata cat de des «bate ceasul» procesorului. Nu spune singur cat de rapid e: doua procesoare de 3 GHz pot lucra foarte diferit.”
