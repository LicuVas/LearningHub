## Executive Summary

This roadmap provides a phased approach to align LearningHub with the Romanian ICT curriculum (grades 5-8) based on comprehensive research of OMEN 3393/2017, DigComp 2.2, and current educational standards.

**Key Insight:** With only 1 hour/week (36 hours/year) of ICT instruction and Romania ranking last in EU for digital competencies, there is a critical need for supplementary learning platforms like LearningHub.

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Curriculum Misalignment
**Mitigation:**
- Engage Romanian education consultant from start
- Regular alignment audits with OMEN 3393/2017
- Pilot testing with teachers and students
- Rapid iteration based on feedback

### Risk 2: Low User Adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Start with pilot schools for validation
- Strong value proposition (free/low-cost, curriculum-aligned)
- Teacher testimonials and case studies
- Partnerships with schools and Ministry
- Referral programs and incentives

### Risk 3: Technical Issues (Performance, Bugs)
**Mitigation:**
- Thorough QA testing before each phase launch
- Phased rollout (not all users at once)
- Monitoring and alerting systems
- Rapid response team for critical issues
- Regular performance optimization

### Risk 4: Competition (Other Platforms)
**Mitigation:**
- Unique value: Alignment with Romanian curriculum (OMEN 3393/2017)
- Focus on underserved market (Romania's digital skills gap)
- Superior UX and content quality
- Community building (teachers, students, parents)
- Continuous innovation and improvement

### Risk 5: Regulatory Changes
**Mitigation:**
- Monitor Ministry of Education announcements
- Flexible content management system (easy updates)
- Maintain relationships with education authorities
- Diversify beyond just curriculum alignment (life skills, projects)
