Concepte intermediare

'Repeta pana cand' (repeat until) - ruleaza pana cand conditia devine adevarata

Diferenta: numar fix (repeat n) vs infinit (forever) vs conditionat (repeat until)

  • 'Repeta pana cand' (repeat until) - ruleaza pana cand conditia devine adevarata
  • Diferenta: numar fix (repeat n) vs infinit (forever) vs conditionat (repeat until)
