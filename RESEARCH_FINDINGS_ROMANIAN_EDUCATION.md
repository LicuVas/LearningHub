# Romanian Education System - ICT Curriculum Research Findings
## Comprehensive Research Document for LearningHub Project

**Project:** LearningHub - ICT Education Platform for Romanian Students (Grades 5-8)
**Live site:** https://learninghub-8z6.pages.dev/
**Research Date:** February 3, 2026
**Compiled by:** Research Agent

---

## Executive Summary

This document compiles comprehensive research on the Romanian education system's ICT curriculum for middle school (gimnaziu) students in grades 5-8. The curriculum is governed by **OMEN 3393/2017** (Ordinul Ministrului Educației Naționale nr. 3393/28.02.2017), which approved the school programs for middle school education.

---

## 1. OFFICIAL CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 Legal Foundation

**Primary Regulatory Document:**
- **OMEN 3393/2017** - Order approving school programs for middle school (gimnaziu)
- Published: Official Monitor no. 164bis, March 7, 2017
- Source: Romanian Ministry of Education (Ministerul Educației)

**Implementation Timeline:**
- **Grade 5:** Implemented starting 2017-2018 school year
- **Grade 6:** Implemented starting 2018-2019 school year
- **Grade 7:** Implemented starting 2019-2020 school year
- **Grade 8:** Implemented starting 2020-2021 school year

### 1.2 Subject Details

**Discipline Name:** Informatică și TIC (Informatics and Information and Communication Technology)

**Classification:**
- Core subject (trunchi comun) for grades 5-8
- **Weekly allocation:** 1 hour per week per grade
- **Annual duration:** 36 weeks (approximately 36 hours per year)

**Curriculum Philosophy:**
The curriculum focuses on:
- Typical information processing processes (not specific software applications)
- Cross-curricular approach with connections to other fields
- Student-centered learning and competency development
- Real-world application and problem-solving
- Transferring and mobilizing knowledge in varied life contexts

## 2. SCHOOL CALENDAR 2025-2026

### 2.1 Academic Year Structure

**Start Date:** September 8, 2025
**End Date:** June 19, 2026
**Total Duration:** 36 weeks of instruction

**Modular System:** The academic year is divided into 5 learning modules separated by 5 vacation periods.

### 2.2 Learning Modules

| Module | Start Date | End Date | Duration |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Module 1 | September 8, 2025 | October 24, 2025 | 7 weeks |
| Module 2 | November 3, 2025 | December 19, 2025 | 7 weeks |
| Module 3 | January 8, 2026 | February 6/13/20, 2026* | 5-6 weeks |
| Module 4 | February 16/23 or March 2, 2026* | April 3, 2026 | 5-6 weeks |
| Module 5 | April 15, 2026 | June 19, 2026 | 10 weeks |

*Note: Dates vary by county/inspectorate (județ)

### 2.3 Vacation Periods

1. **Autumn Break** (After Module 1)
   - October 25 - November 2, 2025 (9 days)

2. **Winter Holiday** (After Module 2)
   - December 20, 2025 - January 7, 2026 (19 days)

3. **Flexible Winter Break** (After Module 3)
   - One week within February 9 - March 1, 2026
   - Decided by county school inspectorates

4. **Spring/Easter Break** (After Module 4)
   - April 4 - April 14, 2026 (11 days)

5. **Summer Vacation** (After Module 5)
   - June 20, 2026 - September 6, 2026 (79 days)

### 2.4 Special Provisions

**Grade 8 Students:**
- Academic year: 35 weeks
- End date: June 12, 2026 (due to national evaluation)

**Grade 12 Students (day program):**
- Academic year: 34 weeks
- End date: June 5, 2026

## 3. CURRICULUM COMPETENCIES

### 3.1 General Competencies Framework

The Informatică și TIC curriculum identifies a relevant set of **general and specific competencies** for contemporary society. General competencies are tracked throughout all middle school years, while specific competencies are derived from general ones and targeted within each academic year.

**Curriculum Foundations:**
The curriculum was developed based on:
- International research documents and policies
- European frameworks (including DigComp references)
- National educational policies
- Romania's Digital Agenda 2020 strategy

**Competency Approach:**
- Focus on acquiring competencies, not just content knowledge
- Emphasis on transferable skills
- Application in varied life contexts
- Development of digital skills for schooling and active life

### 3.2 Key Thematic Areas (Across Grades 5-8)

Based on the curriculum structure, the main thematic areas include:

1. **Computer Systems and Hardware**
   - Primary focus: Grade 5
   - Topics: Hardware components, software components, ergonomics, safety

2. **Algorithms and Programming**
   - Progressive development across all grades
   - Grade 5-6: Graphical programming environments (e.g., Scratch)
   - Grade 7-8: Text-based programming languages

3. **Data and Information Processing**
   - Constants, variables, expressions
   - Operators (arithmetic, relational, logical)
   - String processing

4. **Digital Content Creation**
   - Grade 5-6: Basic digital content
   - Grade 7-8: Web pages, multimedia content
   - Audience-appropriate design and content

5. **Algorithmic Structures**
   - Sequential structures
   - Alternative structures (conditional/decision)
   - Iterative structures (loops)
   - Implementation in programming environments

### 3.3 Grade-Specific Focus Areas

**Grade 5 (Clasa a V-a):**
- Operating systems fundamentals
- Computer hardware and software
- Introduction to Scratch/graphical programming
- Basic algorithmic thinking (sequential structures)
- Workplace ergonomics and safety

**Grade 6 (Clasa a VI-a):**
- Algorithms and graphic animations
- Advanced Scratch programming
- Alternative structures (if-then-else)
- Digital content creation basics

**Grade 7 (Clasa a VII-a):**
- Text-based programming languages
- Advanced algorithmic structures
- String processing
- Data manipulation

**Grade 8 (Clasa a VIII-a):**
- Web page creation
- HTML/CSS fundamentals
- Advanced digital content creation
- Integration and application projects

## 4. DIGITAL COMPETENCIES FRAMEWORK (2024)

### 4.1 National Framework for Digital Competencies

**Regulatory Document:**
- **Ordinul nr. 6466/2024** - Approving the Digital Competencies Framework for Students
- Published: Official Monitor, October 2024
- Effective: 2024-2025 school year

**Reference Model:**
- Based on **DigCompRo** (Romanian adaptation)
- Foundation: **DigComp 2.2** (European Digital Competence Framework)

### 4.2 Six Main Competency Areas

The Digital Competencies Framework covers **six principal areas**:

1. **Information and Data Literacy**
   - Browsing, searching, and filtering data
   - Evaluating data and information
   - Managing data and information

2. **Communication and Collaboration**
   - Interacting through digital technologies
   - Sharing through digital technologies
   - Online collaboration
   - Digital citizenship

3. **Digital Content Creation**
   - Developing digital content
   - Integrating and re-elaborating
   - Copyright and licenses
   - Programming

4. **Safety**
   - Protecting devices
   - Protecting personal data
   - Protecting health and well-being
   - Protecting the environment

5. **Problem Solving**
   - Solving technical problems
   - Identifying needs and responses
   - Using digital tools creatively
   - Identifying digital competence gaps

6. **Responsible and Safe Technology Use**
   - Cybersecurity awareness
   - Ethical use of technology
   - Digital footprint management

### 4.3 Performance Levels

Each competency area includes:
- **Detailed descriptors** for each competency
- **Performance levels** expected at different education stages
- **Specific competencies** broken down by skill area

## 5. ASSESSMENT AND EVALUATION

### 5.1 Lesson Observation Criteria (OMEC 6106/2020)

**Regulatory Document:**
- **OMEC 6106/2020** - Regulation for Inspection of Pre-university Education Units
- Published: Official Monitor, Part I, no. 1281, December 23, 2020

**Key Observation Aspects:**

1. **Teacher-Student Relationship**
   - Systematic application of student-centered teaching strategies
   - Active learning facilitation

2. **Individualization**
   - Customized tasks based on identified needs
   - Differentiated learning situations

3. **Cross-Curricular Approach**
   - Interdisciplinary connections
   - Transdisciplinary lessons/activities

4. **Resource Adequacy**
   - Material resources appropriate to lesson specifics
   - Suitable organization methods

5. **Student Work Display**
   - Presentation of student products in classrooms
   - Use of other school spaces for exhibition

**Inspector Guidelines:**
- No intervention during lesson observation
- Avoid distracting students
- Discuss with teachers and students outside class hours

**Grading System:**
Four qualitative ratings for observed lessons:
- **FB** - Foarte bine (Very Good)
- **B** - Bine (Good)
- **A** - Acceptabil (Acceptable)
- **S** - Slab (Weak)

### 5.2 Digital Competencies Assessment

**Bacalaureat Examination (Starting 2026):**
- **Proba D** - Digital Competencies Test
- **Scheduled:** June 15-17, 2026
- **Nature:** Practical evaluation with concrete tasks in real applications

**Assessment Areas:**
- Document creation and editing
- Spreadsheets and calculations
- Presentations
- Online collaboration tools
- Responsible and safe technology use
- Digital collaboration

**International Equivalency:**
- **ICDL PROFIL BAC** certification can replace the exam
- Over 160,000 high school students opted for ICDL equivalency
- Practical assessment approach aligned with real-world skills

### 5.3 Current Performance Context

**Romania's Position:**
- Consistently ranks last in EU for digital competencies
- **ICILS 2023** study (International Computer and Information Literacy Study)
  - 34 countries participated, including Romania
  - Results published November 2024
  - Highlights need for improved digital literacy and information skills

## 6. TEACHING METHODOLOGY

### 6.1 Pedagogical Approach

**Core Principles:**

1. **Process-Oriented Learning**
   - Focus on information processing processes
   - Not limited to specific software applications
   - Future-proof approach adaptable to technological changes

2. **Cross-Curricular Integration**
   - Connections to other subject areas
   - Real student needs addressed
   - Practical life applications

3. **Activity-Centered Learning**
   - Focus on learning activities and outcomes
   - Student engagement emphasized
   - Competency development through practice

4. **Competency Development**
   - General competencies tracked across all grades
   - Specific competencies per grade/year
   - Progressive skill building

### 6.2 Methodological Guidance

**Official Resources:**
- Methodological guidelines (Repere metodologice) available from Ministry of Education
- Guidance for calendar planning
- Summative assessment examples
- Student project examples
- Open Educational Resources (OER) utilization

**Teacher Flexibility:**
- Teachers establish learning units
- Determine logical sequencing
- Allocate time budgets flexibly
- Adapt to student acquisition levels

### 6.3 Suggested Learning Activities

The curriculum provides:
- **Exemplary learning activities** for each competency
- **Content suggestions** organized by topic
- **Methodological recommendations** for effective teaching
- **Assessment methods** aligned with competencies

**Focus Areas:**
- Practical, hands-on activities
- Project-based learning
- Problem-solving exercises
- Collaborative work
- Digital content creation

## 8. KEY CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES

### 8.1 Identified Challenges

1. **Digital Skills Gap**
   - Romania ranks last in EU for digital competencies
   - Need for intensive competency development
   - ICILS 2023 results highlight gaps

2. **Resource Allocation**
   - Only 1 hour per week for ICT instruction
   - 36 hours per academic year limited for comprehensive coverage
   - Need for efficient, focused curriculum delivery

3. **Assessment Modernization**
   - New digital competencies test (Proba D) starting 2026
   - Transition from theoretical to practical evaluation
   - Need for student preparation in practical skills

### 8.2 Opportunities for LearningHub

1. **Curriculum Alignment**
   - Align platform content with OMEN 3393/2017 competencies
   - Map lessons to specific competencies per grade
   - Provide supplementary resources beyond 1 hour/week

2. **Competency-Based Learning**
   - Implement DigComp 2.2 framework
   - Provide self-assessment tools
   - Track competency development

3. **Practical Skill Development**
   - Interactive exercises and simulations
   - Hands-on projects aligned with curriculum
   - Prepare students for Proba D practical assessment

4. **Personalized Learning**
   - Accommodate varying student levels
   - Individualized learning paths
   - Progress tracking and feedback

5. **Cross-Curricular Integration**
   - Connect ICT to other subjects
   - Real-world problem scenarios
   - Interdisciplinary projects
